Two new ways to show your lists

Settings › General › Show lists as, or the strip's ⋯ menu. Both work with a full-size ribbon along the top or bottom.

  • Stacks replaces the row of pinboards with the current list's name and the lists you use with the app in front. Press Tab to see every list as a stack of cards with its first items; use the arrows and Return to open one, or type to narrow them. Esc returns to the Clipboard.
  • Left rail lists the Clipboard and every pinboard down the side, each one click away.
  • Tab row is the layout you had, and stays the default.

Settings

  • Settings opens in the middle of the display you're on instead of wherever it was last left.
  • The dotted line under the ribbon size slider is gone.
