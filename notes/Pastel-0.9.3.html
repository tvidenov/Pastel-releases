Fast again after days of use

  • Pastel no longer slows down the longer it runs. A Library or Front Page you had closed kept working in the background after every copy, and that work grew over days until opening the ribbon could take seconds. Closed windows now rest until you open them again.
  • The ribbon opens faster: it no longer reads every item's thumbnail again each time you open it, only the cards you see.

Less disk space

  • Settings › General › Keep History shows how much space Pastel takes on this Mac, including pinboards and saved links.
  • Copying a file no longer saves a picture of it alongside, which was often around 10 MB each. Pasting a file only ever used its location. Copies of the same image saved under older clipboard names are gone too.
  • Pictures saved with earlier file copies go as your history expires.

Saved links

  • Deleting a saved link, or a whole collection, now sticks: the daily update from Zen no longer saves the tab again while it's still in Zen's sidebar.
  • Once you close that tab in Zen, opening the page there again later saves it again.
