# Pastel 0.9.0

## Pocket: a compact list at your text cursor

  • Pocket is a small clipboard list that opens right under the text cursor in the app you're typing in. Where an app doesn't share its cursor position, it opens at the pointer. The ribbon stays on ⇧⌘V.
  • Its shortcut: new installs open Pocket with ⌃⌘V. If you're updating, Pocket starts without a shortcut, because ⌃⌘V is Paste Special in Microsoft Office: open it from the menu bar, or set a shortcut in Settings › Shortcuts.
  • Hold to cycle, like ⌘Tab: keep ⌃⌘ down and press V again to step down the list, then let go to paste. Let go after one press and Pocket stays open for searching.
  • Type to search with letters spread out: "ngxrl" finds "nginx reload". Matched letters are highlighted.
  • Paste as: press ⇥ to paste the selected item another way: one line, trimmed, quoted, as code, uppercase, plain text without formatting, a link as Markdown, its title or its domain, a color as RGB, HSL or SwiftUI, a file's path or name, or the text Pastel read in a screenshot.
  • When the intelligent clipboard is on, the item that fits what you're writing is selected, with its reason.
  • Pinboards in the current space appear as chips; switch with ← and →. ⌘1–⌘9 paste by number, ⇧↵ pastes plain text, ⌥⌫ deletes an item, ⎋ clears the search and then closes.

## Saved links

  • Rename saved links: choose Rename… from a link's menu, or press R in the list.
  • Links saved from Zen take the names you gave their tabs in Zen's sidebar, and follow later renames there until you rename them in Pastel. Clearing a name brings back the one from Zen.
