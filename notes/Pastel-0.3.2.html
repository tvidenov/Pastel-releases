Pastel 0.3.2 fixes two Settings issues.

  • "Check for updates automatically" now shows Sparkle's real state instead of appearing off.
  • Accessibility: if Pastel is listed and enabled in System Settings but direct paste still doesn't work (an entry created by a differently signed build), a new "Reset & Ask Again" button in Settings › General and in the paste prompt removes the stale entry and requests access again.

Signed and notarized. Requires macOS 26 or later.
