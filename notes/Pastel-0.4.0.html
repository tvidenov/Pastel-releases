Ask Pastel — on-device Q&A over your clipboard

  • Open the Library (⌘L) and press ⇧⌘A, or use the Ask button, to ask questions like “Which GitHub repos did I save?”, “Find links about AI agents” or “What did I copy this week?”.
  • Answers come from Apple Intelligence running on your Mac; nothing leaves the device. Pastel searches your history and pinboards, cites the items it used as Sources, and follow-up questions keep working (“translate the second one”).
  • Right-click any card and choose “Ask about this…” to ask about a single item, including summarising a link or explaining a code snippet.
  • Turn it off under Settings › Intelligence › Ask Pastel.
