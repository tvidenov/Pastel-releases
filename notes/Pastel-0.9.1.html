Put the ribbon where you want it

  • Choose where the ribbon opens in Settings › General › Ribbon, or from the ribbon's ⋯ menu: Bottom (as before), Top, Notch, Left or Right.
  • Notch hangs a shelf from the camera notch, about four cards wide, that grows out of the notch when it opens. On a display without a notch, it hangs from the top center.
  • Top spans the screen over the menu bar, with the cards starting below the notch.
  • Left and Right show a column of cards down the side of the screen, with their own width. ↑ and ↓ move through them.
  • Drag the ribbon's inner edge to resize it in any position. The preview opens beside the ribbon, and confirmations move below a ribbon at the top.
  • Open by hovering over the notch (off by default): rest the pointer on the notch, or the top center of a display without one, to open a ribbon at the top. It closes when the pointer leaves, unless you click it.

Faster with big copies

  • The ribbon opens right away even when you've just copied something huge, like a long base64 string. Before, it could take a third of a second every time.
  • Copying very large text no longer makes Pastel stall for a moment.
  • The preview keeps its full size on a display to the left of your main one.
